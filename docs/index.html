Cottage Sort

A calm colour-sorting puzzle with a cottage to rebuild.

Pour the coloured beads from jar to jar until each one holds a single colour. Every puzzle you clear earns coins, and every coin goes into restoring a little cottage that has seen better days.

No timers, no lives, no pressure. Play at your own pace, offline. Your progress lives on your device.
