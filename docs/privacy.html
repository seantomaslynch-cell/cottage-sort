Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Cottage Sort (“the game”, “we”) is a single-player puzzle game. It has no account and no sign-in. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and to show ads.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress — levels cleared, stars, coins, gems, cottage upgrades, decor, and settings — is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the advertising service collects

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google, which has its own privacy policy. Through AdMob the following may be collected:

DataPurposeLinked to your identityUsed to track you
Advertising identifier (IDFA)Show and measure adsNoYes
Ad interaction / usage dataShow and measure adsNoYes
Approximate location (from IP address)Show region-appropriate adsNoYes

On iOS you are asked for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the first ad request. If you decline, ads are served in a non-personalised mode.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, purchase history, search history, health or financial information, or any content you create. The game has no login and no first-party analytics server — any in-game event log stays on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Where a user is flagged as a child, ads are served in a non-personalised mode.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising identifier, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.
  • For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app’s release notes.
