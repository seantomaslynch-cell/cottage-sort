# Privacy Policy — Cottage Sort

_Last updated: 2026-08-29. This is a template; a real build must host it at a
public URL (see `Config.PRIVACY_POLICY_URL`) and have counsel review it._

Cottage Sort ("the game", "we") is a single-player puzzle game. We keep data
collection to the minimum needed to run the game, show ads, and process
purchases.

## What the game itself stores

- **On your device only:** your progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, gems,
  cottage upgrades, decor, settings). This never leaves your device and is not
  sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What third parties collect

The game uses these services. Each has its own privacy policy.

| Service | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob (ads) | Advertising ID, device info, approximate location (from IP), ad interaction | Show and measure ads |
| Apple App Store / Google Play (billing) | Purchase transaction data | Process and restore in-app purchases |
| [Analytics provider] | App events, session length, device model, crash logs — not linked to your identity | Improve the game and fix bugs |

- We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos,
  precise location, or account credentials. The game has no login.
- Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).

## Your choices

- **Ads:** buy "Remove interstitial ads" to stop full-screen ads (opt-in
  rewarded videos remain). Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad
  personalization in your device settings.
- **Analytics:** [describe an opt-out if the chosen SDK supports one].
- **Data deletion:** uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. For any
  data held by the third parties above, use their controls or contact
  `Config.SUPPORT_EMAIL`.

## Children

The game is rated for all ages but is not directed at children under 13 and
does not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are served in a
non-personalized mode where required (e.g. for users flagged as children).
