- Everything else: "Data not linked to you".

## Google Play "Data safety" answers

- **Data collected:** Device or other IDs (ads), Purchase history, App
  interactions, Crash logs, App performance.
- **Data shared:** Device or other IDs — shared with the ad network for
  advertising.
- **Encrypted in transit:** Yes.
- **Can users request deletion:** Yes — uninstall removes on-device data;
  contact for third-party data.
- **Committed to Play Families policy:** only if you target children (you don't
  by default).
